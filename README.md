# Furniture Style Prototypes

**One live demo with 4 fully clickable furniture website styles.**  
Built as a sales tool so furniture workshops and sellers in Aba (and across Nigeria) can see options and choose the style that fits their business.

🔗 **Live demo:** [https://furniture-style-prototypes.vercel.app](https://furniture-style-prototypes.vercel.app)

---

## The problem

Most furniture businesses in Aba still rely on WhatsApp photos and word of mouth.  
When they want a website, they often cannot picture the difference between a simple page, a shop, a workshop site, or a premium look — so they delay or pick the wrong thing.

This prototype solves that in one link: open it, walk through each style, and decide.

---

## What’s inside

Four complete, navigable website styles in a single project:

| Style | Best for | Feel |
|-------|----------|------|
| **Maker / Workshop** | Producers who build custom pieces | Warm wood tones, craftsmanship, “order custom” focus |
| **Shop / E-commerce** | Sellers who want a catalog and clear prices | Clean product grid, WhatsApp order buttons |
| **Luxury Showroom** | Higher-end furniture and interior clients | Dark, minimal, gallery-style, showroom visit CTA |
| **Simple Portfolio** | Small makers who just need a phone-friendly page | Minimal, fast, photo + WhatsApp only |

Every style has its own Home → Products/Collections → About → Contact flow.  
Real furniture images are used so the pages already look serious when a client opens them.

---

## How to use it with clients

1. Send the live link on WhatsApp or call.
2. Let the client click into each style and “walk around”.
3. Ask which direction they prefer.
4. Build their real site in that style (or a mix).

This turns a vague “I need a website” conversation into a clear choice.

## Tech stack

- **Next.js** (App Router)
- **TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS**
- Deployed on **Vercel**

Images are served from Unsplash (free for commercial use).
